# The OBSD Case Writing Kit

Turn a news article into a classroom-ready dilemma teaching case — with any AI assistant, including free ones.

## Whose work this is

The OBSD method (**O**bjective, **B**ackground, **S**ituation, **D**ilemma) and the case-writing rules in this kit were developed by:

- **Dr. Andrew Schenkel** — Stockholm School of Economics
- **Dr. Ciara Sutton** — Stockholm School of Economics
- **Dr. Olof Brunninge** — Jönköping International Business School

This repository hosts their method in copy-paste form so that any educator, anywhere, can use it with whatever AI tool they have — including free chatbots and small local models. Hosting and prompt engineering by [NegoAI](https://github.com/NegoAI); all credit for the method and pedagogy belongs to the authors.

## Get started

Open **[OBSD-case-writing-kit.md](OBSD-case-writing-kit.md)**. It contains everything:

- **The Full Version** — paste into free ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or DeepSeek (or save as Project/Gem/GPT instructions). The AI interviews you through the OBSD questions, stress-tests your dilemma, writes the case, and shows where every fact came from.
- **The Light Version** — for small or local AI models. You answer the questions yourself, the AI only writes, and you verify the facts with a short checklist.
- **Three repair prompts** for when a case comes out wrong, an optional **instructor's epilogue** step, and a **complete worked example** from article to finished case.

First time? Read the worked example at the end of the kit before anything else — it is faster than any instruction.

Keep the kit as plain text or markdown and copy from it directly. Do not copy from a PDF.

## Contribute a case

Cases that worked in a real classroom are welcome in [`cases/`](cases/) — see [cases/README.md](cases/README.md) for the simple format. Open a pull request, or open an issue with your files attached if you prefer.
