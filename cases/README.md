# The OBSD case bank

Cases built with the kit that worked in a real classroom. One folder per case.

## How to contribute

Add a folder named after your case (for example `cases/river-port-strike/`) containing:

1. **`source.md`** — a link to the article(s) the case is built from, plus the date you accessed them. Do not include the article text itself unless you have the right to share it.
2. **`worksheet.md`** — your answers to the 16 OBSD questions.
3. **`case.md`** — the finished case (750–1,000 words), after your own fact-check pass.
4. **`teaching-note.md`** *(optional)* — board plan, opening questions, discussion flow.
5. At the top of `case.md`, note: the course level it was used with (undergraduate / MBA / executive), the language, and whether names or details were disguised.

Then open a pull request — or, if you prefer, open an issue and attach your files.

## The quality bar

Before contributing, your case should pass the kit's own checks: every fact traceable to your source, both options defensible, no obvious right answer, and an ending at the exact moment of decision, revealing nothing.

Cases are shared under the repository's CC BY 4.0 license, credited to you as the case writer, with the OBSD method credited to its authors.
