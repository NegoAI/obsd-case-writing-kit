# obsd-case-writing-kit

## What this repo is

The OBSD Case Writing Kit: copy-paste prompts that turn a news article into a classroom dilemma teaching case with any AI assistant. The method belongs to Schenkel, Sutton and Brunninge; hosting and prompt engineering by NegoAI. Remote: `NegoAI/obsd-case-writing-kit`. Licensed CC BY 4.0.

## Where things live

- `OBSD-case-writing-kit.md`: the kit (full version, light version, repair prompts, worked example).
- `cases/README.md`: format for contributed cases.

## This repo's limits

- The method stays credited to its three authors (`README.md`).
- Keep the kit as plain text or markdown that can be copied from directly; no PDF copies.
